The Lore: The Castle Keep & The Wandering Spirits
Instead of dark demonic entities attacking your machine, your macOS system is a fortified castle courtyard, and unprivileged child processes are mischievous, curious spirits trying to slip past the portcullis gates into protected vaults (~/Documents, Contacts, Camera).
Your app—Gatekeeper’s Ward—acts as the night watch lantern, spotting these playful phantoms suspended mid-escape while macOS holds the gate shut.
